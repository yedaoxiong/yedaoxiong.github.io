MiniBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · MiniBook 1.1.0

简体中文 繁體中文 English

MiniBook is a local-first, minimalist bookkeeping app. We value the privacy of your financial records and explain clearly how the app handles data.

1Data collection

MiniBook requires no account and includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. The app does not automatically upload your ledgers, categories, transactions, balances, or notes to the developer or sell that data. Network information involved in reference rates and feedback you choose to send are described in sections 4 and 9.

2On-device data

Ledger names and currencies, opening balances, categories, entries, notes, language, main currency, appearance, last successful backup time, and cached rates are stored on your device. MiniBook has no account system and does not initiate automatic cloud sync. Device-level backup and restore are managed by iOS and your system settings.

3Export, backup, and restore

MiniBook creates a CSV export or JSON backup only when you ask it to. The system share sheet lets you choose where to save it or who receives it. Restore reads only the MiniBook backup file you explicitly select. Once a file leaves the app, it is handled under the rules of the location or third-party service you choose.

4Exchange-rate updates

When different-currency ledgers exist, MiniBook requests public reference rates over HTTPS from the open.er-api.com endpoint of ExchangeRate-API. The business parameter is the ISO code of the main currency. Ledger names, balances, entries, categories, notes, and advertising identifiers are not sent. Amounts are converted on your device.

The network connection exposes technical information such as IP address and request time to the provider and its infrastructure. Their processing is covered by the provider's Terms and Privacy Policy. MiniBook does not control third-party server logs or retention periods, and does not use these requests for advertising or user profiling.

Successful results are cached for about 24 hours per main currency. Expired or failed requests may be retried, and changing the main currency may trigger another request. Available cached rates remain usable offline; expired rates are labeled as cached when a network refresh fails. Without rates, original-currency records remain usable. Estimates do not change saved amounts or represent a bank or payment provider's settlement rate. If all ledgers use the main currency, cross-currency conversion does not trigger rate requests.

5System access and external links

MiniBook uses the system document picker and share sheet for export and restore. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, or health data. The app opens the system browser when you choose Privacy, Support, or the exchange-rate source. From the Support page, you may separately choose an external contact method such as email or Weibo. No ledger data is attached to these links.

6Data deletion

You can delete entries, categories, and ledgers in the app. Category deletion also removes associated entries; ledger deletion also removes its entries. MiniBook explains the impact and asks for confirmation. Choosing Delete App in iOS removes the app's local data; Offload App may retain it. System backups and copies of exports or backups in Files or other locations must be managed or deleted separately.

7Third-party components

The app uses Flutter and components for a local database, preferences, network requests, document selection, system sharing, and external links. Other than the exchange-rate request described above, the current version does not use these components to transmit user data to third parties.

8Children's privacy

MiniBook is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9Updates and contact

If a future version adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other data features, this policy will be updated before release. For questions, email yedaoxiong@qq.com or visit Support and Feedback. If you send feedback, we receive the email address and content you provide to respond to your request. Please avoid sending real financial, identity, or other sensitive information.
