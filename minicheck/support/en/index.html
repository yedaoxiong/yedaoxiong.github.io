Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Projects, check-in records, individual check-in times, and interface preferences are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to a server. In Settings → Data and backup, you can review records or create a file backup.

How do I delete my data?

You can archive a project from its edit screen. Uninstalling MiniCheck removes all local data the app stores on that device. Create a full backup first if you want to keep it.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. MiniCheck has no account or login feature, and its core check-in features work offline. A connection is needed only when you explicitly open an external feedback page.

How do I export a check-in image?

Open All Projects Month or Single Year and tap the export button. MiniCheck creates one complete long image that you can save to Photos or share with the system share sheet.

How do I back up or move all my check-in data?

Open Settings → Data and backup, choose Create full backup, and save the JSON file to Files. On another device, install MiniCheck, choose Restore from backup, and open that file. Restoring replaces the projects and check-in records currently on that device.

How can I review exact check-in times?

Open Settings → Check-in history to view individual check-in times by date and project. Totals created before check-in history was introduced are preserved, but their original times cannot be reconstructed.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include what happened, steps to reproduce it, your iPhone model, and iOS version. If possible, attach a screenshot that contains no personal or sensitive information.
