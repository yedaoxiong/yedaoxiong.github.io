MiniDays Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

简体中文 繁體中文 English

MiniDays is a local-first app for important dates. Your anniversaries, countdowns, and expiry dates remain under your control. This policy explains clearly how the app handles data.

1Data collection

The current version of MiniDays requires no account and includes no advertising or analytics. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your personal data, usage data, dates, or notes with third parties.

2On-device data

Anniversaries, countdowns, expiry dates, notes, amounts, renewal settings, icons, and colors are stored in the app's local database on your current device. Language, theme color, and display preferences also remain on the device. The current version has no account system or cloud sync.

3Local notifications

MiniDays uses iOS local notifications only after you select reminders for an expiry date and allow notifications. Reminder content is created and scheduled on your device, does not pass through a developer server, and does not use remote push. You can disable notifications at any time in system settings.

4System permissions and external services

The current version requests notification permission only when you enable expiry reminders. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data. The app integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, account, or cloud-storage service. Apple may process App Store, system notification, and diagnostic data under its own policies.

5Data deletion

You can delete individual items in the app or clear local data by category under Settings → Data Management. Related local reminders are cancelled. Uninstalling MiniDays removes the local data stored by the app on that device. The current version has no backup or restore feature, so the developer cannot recover deleted data.

6Third-party components

The app uses Flutter and components for a local database, local preferences, version information, time zones, and system notifications. The current version does not use these components to transmit your dates or notes to the developer.

7Children's privacy

MiniDays is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8Policy updates and contact

If a future version adds cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other data features, this policy will be updated before those features are released. For questions, email yedaoxiong@qq.com or visit the Support and Feedback page.
