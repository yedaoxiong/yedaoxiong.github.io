Frequently asked questions

Where are my dates and notes stored?

Anniversaries, countdowns, expiry dates, notes, renewal settings, and interface preferences are stored locally on your current device. MiniDays requires no account and does not sync your data to the cloud.

What is the difference between the three categories?

Anniversaries are for important dates that repeat yearly and show both the next occurrence and elapsed time. Countdowns are for one-time goals such as trips, exams, or launches. Expiry dates track memberships, domains, documents, warranties, and other deadlines that may need renewal or attention.

Why did an expiry reminder not appear?

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → MiniDays and confirm that notifications are allowed. Also check that the item includes a reminder date that has not passed. Reminders are delivered as on-device local notifications at 9:00 a.m. on the selected date.

How do manual, automatic, and non-renewing items differ?

Manual renewal is for something you need to pay or handle yourself. Automatic renewal indicates an expected automatic charge. Non-renewing items track a single expiry. Tap Renewed to calculate the next date using a monthly, yearly, or custom cycle.

How can I review or clear local data?

Open Settings → Data Management to review item counts and clear each category. Expiry dates can also be archived, then restored or permanently removed under Archived Expiry Dates.

Can I recover data after deleting the app?

No. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. The current version of MiniDays does not include backup or restore, so make sure you no longer need the records before deleting the app.

What should I include when reporting a problem?

Please include what happened, steps to reproduce it, the MiniDays version, your iPhone model, and iOS version. If you attach screenshots, remove any dates or notes you do not want to share.
